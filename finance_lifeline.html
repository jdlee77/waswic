About this beta

This is an estimate, not ground truth. It infers card-payment capability from the status of three dependencies — processor/card network, local power, and comms — not from any real transaction data. No transaction data of any kind is collected or shown.

Payment capability for an area = the worst of its three dependency statuses. Processor status is panel-wide (national); power and comms are set per county/area below.

Oregon ships with real county centers and a full offline fallback. Every other state and territory relies on a live fetch of county boundaries from the US Census Bureau's TIGERweb service — if that's unreachable (no internet, or this sandbox), that area's counties won't list until it is.

This is a focused beta build of CommOps's Finance Lifeline module, for testing that module on its own.
